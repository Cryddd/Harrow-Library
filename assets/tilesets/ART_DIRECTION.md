# Tileset Art Direction

Use the reference images as the quality bar: warm wall trim, readable top-down perspective, thick silhouettes, and dense environmental storytelling.

## Tile Sets

- `floor1_tiles.png`: warm library wood, brick lower walls, reception trim, cozy carpets, plants, bookshelves, lamps, student furniture.
- `floor2_tiles.png`: modern lab flooring, glass partitions, server racks, workstations, presentation stage, robotics and AI research props.
- `floor3_tiles.png`: restricted archive walls, damaged lab floor, purple system glow, abandoned desks, redacted whiteboards, sealed research doors.

## Rules

- Keep walkable tiles clean and readable.
- Use detail density on walls, desks, shelves, and corners.
- Every room needs one landmark visible at a glance.
- Avoid mirrored layouts unless the room purpose requires order, such as lab rows.
- Use lighting props to guide players toward interactables.
